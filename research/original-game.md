# The original Helbreath (Siementech, 1999-2005)

Researched 2026-10-25. Private-server material excluded. No official manual or archived
official site could be reached (Wayback, Wikipedia, Fandom blocked the fetch), so almost
everything here is third-party. Labels: **[news]** contemporary trade press (Gamemeca),
**[mirror]** mirror of the en Wikipedia article (community-edited, partly opinion),
**[fan-FAQ]** 2003 fan FAQ, **[forum]** player posts, **[unverified]** snippet or single claim.

The best primary source for mechanics is the v2.20 source itself (see `sources.md`): the
server's `Item.cfg`, `Magic.cfg`, `Skill.cfg`, `Npc.cfg`, `Quest.cfg`, `Crusade.cfg` and
`Game.cpp` define the actual rules of that build.

## Functional

- PC MMORPG by Siementech (Seoul). Windows, DirectX 7; min spec P-II 400 MHz, 128 MB RAM,
  8 MB video. [mirror] 2D isometric, Diablo-like presentation (Gamemeca calls it "3D";
  likely loose usage). [third-party 2016, news]
- Client + publisher-run servers per region: Korea, International, USA, Asia, China, Japan,
  Taiwan. [mirror]
- Publishers: Siementech (Korea, International); iEntertainment Network (USA, franchise)
  [mirror]; Gamania (Taiwan, announced Jul 2001) [news]; Terra Korea / Digital Adventure
  "Net Game Center" (Japan, beta 1 Sep 2002, commercial 1 Oct 2002) [news]. China and Taiwan
  were in commercial service by Aug 2002. [news]
- Business model: subscription. USA about $9.95/month, 5-day trial later 14-day [forum,
  unverified]. Servers went pay-to-play after level-cap raises (no date). [mirror]
- Scale: about 13,516 overseas accounts by 23 Oct 2001 with no overseas marketing [news];
  "1.2 million international accounts, ~125,000 US" in Jan 2003 [unverified snippet, WRAL].
- Shutdown of the official service: date not found.

## Mechanical

- **Two nations.** New players are neutral Travellers in a starter zone and cannot pass
  level 19 without citizenship. Aresden (red, god Aresien) vs Elvine (blue, Eldiniel); the
  other nation is a permanent enemy. [mirror, fan-FAQ]
- **Civilian vs Combatant.** Civilians cannot be attacked by enemy players (monsters still
  can); protection limit is stated inconsistently (level 100). [mirror]
- **Stats.** STR, VIT, DEX, INT, MAG, CHR; 3 points per level. STR: melee damage, load
  (100 weight per point), stamina. DEX: hit, dodge, defence. VIT: HP and regen. INT: cast
  chance, spell access. MAG: mana, spell damage, magic resist. CHR: shop prices, needed to
  lead a guild. [mirror]
- **Skills** (use-based, each max 100%, total over 700% makes others decay). Caps tied to
  stats: STR x2 hand attack/mining/manufacturing; DEX x2 swords/bow/axe/shield/fishing/
  hammer; MAG x2 magic/staff; INT x2 alchemy/pretend-corpse; VIT x2 poison resist; level x2
  magic resist. Gathering and crafting were the main money sources. [fan-FAQ]
- **Magic.** 10 circles by INT (circle 1 about 20 INT: Magic Missile, Create Food, Heal;
  circle 5 about 50: Firewall, Lightning; circle 10 about 195: Mass Illusion, Blizzard).
  Rain and hunger lower cast chance. [fan-FAQ]
- **PvP.** Ctrl+click to attack players in attackable zones; spells auto-hit. Enemy Kill
  (EK) points buy account-bound Hero armour. Killing your own citizens gives a criminal
  count (hostile to all until it decays) and negative reputation. [fan-FAQ, mirror]
- **Death.** Lose experience and some items (Travellers exempt); respawn at home. [fan-FAQ]
- **Items.** Primary + secondary attributes, mostly from monster drops. Level cap rose
  120 -> 130 -> 140 -> 160 -> 180 over the service life; max-level gear (Dark Knight / Dark
  Wizard) upgraded with Majestic Points. [mirror] (Later-era features; not in 2.20.)
- **Crusade.** GM-started war: break the enemy town's shields, build a base in Middleland,
  call a meteor strike on the enemy town. Announced as the "Helbreath Crusade" expansion
  Jul 2001 with warrior / builder / commander roles. [news, mirror] USA ran it weekly; castle
  sieges later alternated weekly. [mirror]
- **World.** Two towns, neutral areas, Middleland battlefield, dungeons (North Dungeon was
  a level 20-79 PvP spot). [mirror, fan-FAQ]
- **Not found from non-private sources:** criticals, guild rules, quest/NPC/monster lists,
  Heldenian rules (likely post-2.20 anyway).

## Social

- The town war was the identity: constant cross-nation fights, ambushes, roaming PvP.
  [forum]
- Early PvP chased hero capes, rare drops and zemstones. [mirror]
- USA, ~2005: small population, rough chat culture, scamming, account sharing, trade-driven
  gold economy; the US operator could not change drop rates and needed Korean approval for
  fixes; GM moderation seen as inconsistent. [forum]
- Decline is blamed on level-cap raises, pay-to-play, balance (Blizzard, hammers), the
  "Apocalypse" update and rampant hacking (speed hacks, dupes). [mirror, opinion]
- Private servers appeared from 2002 after a reported ex-employee leak; iEnt sent
  cease-and-desist letters from May 2006. [mirror]
