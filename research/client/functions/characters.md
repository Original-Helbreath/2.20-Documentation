## Overview

- `DrawObjects` walks visible cells in painter's order and calls one `DrawObject_On<Action>` per owner (stop, move, run, attack, magic, damage, dying, dead, get item). Each composes the body plus equipment layers from the appearance words (sprite ranges in `..\assets.md`), dye colours, shadows, names and chat bubbles; details in `..\rendering\frame-pipeline.md`.
- `_iGetFOE` decides friend/enemy colouring, `DrawNpcName`/`GetNpcName` names NPCs, `_SetIlusionEffect` swaps appearance for illusion. Text helpers (`PutString*`) live here because they are used mostly for names and chat.
