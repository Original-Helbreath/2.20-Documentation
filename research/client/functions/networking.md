## Overview

- **Sockets** (verified): two `XSocket`s, `m_pGSock` (game server) and `m_pLSock` (log server), non-blocking with `WSAAsyncSelect` to window messages `WM_USER+2000` / `WM_USER+2001` (defined without parentheses, Game.h:101-102: a precedence hazard in expressions), routed to `OnGameSocketEvent` / `OnLogSocketEvent`. `XSocket` frames each message with a 3-byte header (key byte + 16-bit size) and keeps an unsent-data queue (`_iRegisterUnsentData`/`_iSendUnsentData`). Wire format: `..\..\shared\messages\`.
- **Framing and cipher** (verified, XSocket.cpp:465-501 iSendMsg, 589-611 pGetRcvDataPointer; send at 488-489, receive reverses at 606-607): byte 0 = key, bytes 1-2 = total size (payload+3), payload from byte 3. If key != 0 each payload byte i is transformed b += (i ^ key); b ^= (key ^ (size - i)); receive reverses it. `iSendMsg` queues to the unsent list when a send is pending.
- **Inbound game** (verified): `GameRecvMsgHandler` (Game.cpp:2936) switches on the MSGID dword: init-player/init-data responses, motion responses and events, `MSGID_NOTIFY` to `NotifyMsgHandler` (Game.cpp:29946, 133 `case DEF_NOTIFY_*` plus 4 `case DEF_COMMONTYPE_*`, 29946-31462, most delegating to `NotifyMsg_*`), common events, teleport lists, panning, NPC talk (via MSGID_NOTIFY), fightzone reservation.
- **Inbound log** (verified): `LogResponseHandler` (Game.cpp:14773) handles `DEF_LOGRESMSGTYPE_*` replies; see `..\..\servers\client-log-protocol.md`.
- **Outbound** (verified): `bSendCommand` (Game.cpp:1431) is the builder for 27 distinct `case MSGID_*` (28 labels; REQUEST_CHANGEPASSWORD appears twice under `#if`; 34 case labels in total including 2 DEF_COMMONTYPE and 4 DEF_XSOCKEVENT result cases); one exception: `RequestFullObjectData` builds and sends its packet inline (Game.cpp:8650-8664); it packs the command, direction, coordinates and up to five int/string args per MSGID. `ConnectionEstablishHandler` (Game.cpp:3030) sends the first request after connect.
- **Keep-alive** (verified): `MSGID_COMMAND_CHECKCONNECTION` every 8 s from `OnTimer`; the disconnect is separate: every 2 s in `OnTimer`, if `m_cCommandCount >= 6` then `++m_iNetLagCount`, and at 7 the client goes to `ONCONNECTIONLOST` (Game.cpp:4288-4301). The counter counts unanswered motion commands, not keep-alive replies. `_CalcSocketClosed` (34749) has no caller found and handles the billing socket closing.
- `RequestTeleportAndWaitData` blocks enemy-nation buildings outside crusade (GlobalDef.h:78-80 change log, verified as text).
